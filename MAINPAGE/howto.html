How to Play Torpedo!


  1. Wait for Another Player: After launching the game, you’ll enter a waiting room. The match begins once another player joins.

  2. Place Your Ships: You’ll be presented with a 10x10 grid. Place your ships strategically—each ship takes up exactly 1 square on the grid. Choose your positions wisely.

  3. Gameplay Begins: Once both players have placed their ships, the game starts. Take turns selecting grid squares to launch torpedoes. The goal is to locate and destroy all of your opponent’s ships before they find yours.
